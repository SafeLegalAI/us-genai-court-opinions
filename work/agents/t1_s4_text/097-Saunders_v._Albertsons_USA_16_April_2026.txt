      District Court for the District of Colorado. 28 U.S.C. §§ 636(b)(1)(A), (B); Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(a), (b). Failure to make any
      such objection will result in a waiver of the right to appeal the Magistrate Judge's order or recommendation. See Sinclair
      Wyo. Ref. Co. v. A & B Builders, Ltd., 989 F.3d 747, 783 (10th Cir. 2021) (firm waiver rule applies to non-dispositive
      orders); but see Morales-Fernandez v. INS, 418 F.3d 1116, 1119, 1122 (10th Cir. 2005) (firm waiver rule does not apply
      when the interests of justice require review, including when a “pro se litigant has not been informed of the time period
      for objecting and the consequences of failing to object”).
