21   Respondents are not aware of any authority entitling Petitioner to a
     bond hearing.” (ECF 12 at 2.) “In our adversarial system of
22   adjudication, we follow the principle of party presentation” meaning
23   that “in both civil and criminal cases, in the first instance and on
     appeal . . . we rely on the parties to frame the issues for decision and
24   assign to courts the role of neutral arbiter of matters the parties
25   present.” United States v. Sineneng-Smith, 590 U.S. 371, 375 (2020)
     (citation modified). Further, “as a general rule, our system is designed
26
     around the premise that [parties represented by competent counsel]
27   know what is best for them, and are responsible for advancing the facts
28   and argument entitling them to relief.” Id. at 375–76 (citation
     modified). Thus, the Court grants the agreed-upon relief.
