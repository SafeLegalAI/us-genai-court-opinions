 1          Pursuant to the Court’s January 30, 2024 Order (ECF No. 56, “Order”), Plaintiff Jonathan

 2 Faridian and Defendant DoNotPay, Inc. (“DNP” or “Company”) hereby jointly submit this Joint

 3 Case Status Update.

 4          On March 3, 2023, Plaintiff filed a complaint against Defendant in California state court.

 5 See Faridian v. DoNotPay, Inc., Case No. CGC-23-604987.

 6          On April 7, 2023, Defendant timely removed this action to this Court on diversity and CAFA

 7 grounds. ECF No. 1.

 8          On April 17, 2023, Plaintiff filed a Motion for Leave to Conduct Jurisdictional Discovery.

 9 ECF No. 18 (“Jurisdictional Motion”).

10          On April 28, 2023, Defendant moved for an order compelling Plaintiff to arbitrate his claims

11 pursuant to the Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. §§ 1-16. ECF No. 30 (“Motion to Compel”),

12 which the Court initially determined would be resolved on the papers. ECF No. 47.

13          On November 29, 2023, this case was reassigned from Judge James D. Donato to Judge Rita

14 F. Lin (ECF No. 49) and the parties re-noticed the Jurisdictional Motion and Motion to Compel for

15 hearing on January 30, 2024 pursuant to the Court’s order. ECF No. 50.

16          During the January 30, 2024 hearing, the Court expressed concern that the current record

17 did not allow the Court to determine whether it had jurisdiction to rule on the Motion to Compel

18 and directed the parties to conduct limited jurisdictional discovery and, if necessary, to augment the

19 record and/or that Plaintiff could move for remand. See ECF No. 56.
20          On February 16, 2024, DNP provided Plaintiff with a Declaration of Andrew Kim, DNP’s

21 Vice President of Product, to answer questions posed by Plaintiff regarding jurisdiction. See Ex. 1.

22 On February 23, 2024, DNP provided a Supplemental Declaration of Andrew Kim, regarding

23 Plaintiff’s remaining questions. See Ex. 2. On February 26, 2024, Plaintiff’s counsel responded

24 requesting a 30(b)(6) deposition. See E-mails Between Counsel, Ex. 3. Defendant’s counsel

25 responded on February 28, 2024, offering Mr. Kim to sit for a limited 30(b)(6) deposition. Id.

26          Both Parties have accordingly agreed to a 30(b)(6) deposition, with the expectation that the

27 deposition will resolve the remaining questions on jurisdictional discovery. The one remaining point

28 of dispute between the Parties is the length of that deposition: Defendant has offered one hour, and
