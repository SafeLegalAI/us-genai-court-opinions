 1          Pursuant to the Court’s April 1, 2024 Order (ECF No. 68, “Order”), Plaintiff Jonathan

 2 Faridian and Defendant DoNotPay, Inc. (“DoNotPay”) hereby jointly submit this Joint Case Status

 3 Update.

 4          On March 3, 2023, Plaintiff filed a complaint against Defendant in California state court.

 5 See Faridian v. DoNotPay, Inc., Case No. CGC-23-604987 (Cal. Super. Ct.).

 6          On April 7, 2023, Defendant timely removed this action to this Court on diversity and Class

 7 Action Fairness Act grounds. ECF No. 1.

 8          On April 17, 2023, Plaintiff filed a Motion for Leave to Conduct Jurisdictional Discovery.

 9 ECF No. 18 (“Jurisdictional Motion”).

10          On April 28, 2023, Defendant moved for an order compelling Plaintiff to arbitrate his claims

11 pursuant to the Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. §§ 1-16. ECF No. 30 (“Motion to Compel”), which

12 the Court initially determined would be resolved on the papers. ECF No. 47.

13          On November 29, 2023, this case was reassigned from Judge James D. Donato to Judge Rita

14 F. Lin (ECF No. 49) and the Parties re-noticed the Jurisdictional Motion and Motion to Compel for

15 hearing on January 30, 2024 pursuant to the Court’s order. ECF No. 50.

16          During the January 30, 2024 hearing, the Court expressed concern that the current record

17 did not allow the Court to determine whether it had jurisdiction to rule on the Motion to Compel

18 and directed the Parties to conduct limited jurisdictional discovery and, if necessary, to augment the

19 record and/or that Plaintiff could move for remand. See ECF No. 56.
20          Following DoNotPay’s submission of declarations, the Parties have agreed to a 30(b)(6)

21 deposition, with the expectation that the deposition will resolve the remaining questions on

22 jurisdictional discovery. Plaintiff’s position is that discovery to this point does not resolve questions

23 sufficient to allow this Court to assert jurisdiction. Defendant disagrees but has consented to the

24 30(b)(6) deposition. However, due to ongoing settlement discussions, the deposition has not yet

25 taken place.

26          On March 29, 2024, the Plaintiff requested, with DoNotPay’s consent, that the Court

27 continue the date for status or moving for remand due to ongoing settlement discussions. ECF No.

28 66. The Court granted this request on April 1, 2024. ECF No. 68.
